1. What Z Mic Studio Does
Z Mic Studio turns your Android phone into a studio-grade microphone for your Windows PC over your local Wi-Fi network. Audio travels strictly over your local network between your phone and your computer.

2. Acceptable Use
Please do not use this app to:
- Pretend to be another person or hide who you really are.
- Record people without their permission.
- Break any law, including local privacy and recording laws.
The creator cannot be responsible for recordings you make with this software.

3. Privacy
- Your audio never leaves your local network. It is never uploaded to any cloud or third-party server.
- This app has no user accounts, no advertising, no telemetry, and no tracking.
- Updates are checked only against the official GitHub Releases page without sending personal information.
